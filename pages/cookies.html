Cookie policy

Effective {{updated}}

The short version: GiftingSmart doesn't set any cookies, and doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. It keeps a few items in your browser's local storage so the app works, which is why there's no cookie banner.

What we store in your browser

Local storage is a small storage area in your browser that only this website can read. Nothing below is shared with other websites or used to track you.

NamePurposeHow long
sb-…-auth-tokenKeeps you signed in (set by our sign-in provider, Supabase).Until you sign out
sb-…-auth-token-code-verifierA one-time security code used while you sign in with Google.Removed once sign-in completes
giftbudget-themeYour light or dark mode choice, if you've made one.Until you change it
giftbudget-currencyThe currency you chose for showing amounts.Until you change it
giftbudget-browser-alertsWhether you turned on browser notifications for price drops.Until you change it
giftbudget-shown-alertsWhich price alerts have already popped up, so you don't see them twice.Until you clear it
giftbudget-next (session storage)Remembers the page you were going to while you sign in.Until you close the tab
giftbudget-demo-v1Demo mode only: sample data kept in your browser.Until you sign out of the demo

These items are strictly necessary for features you ask for, so they don't require consent under Canadian privacy law. Fonts and app code are served from our own site, so no third party learns that you visited.

Signing in with Google

When you choose "Continue with Google", you go to Google's sign-in page, where Google may use its own cookies under Google's privacy policy. That happens only when you sign in.

Removing stored items

  • Signing out removes your session.
  • You can clear everything at any time in your browser's settings (look for "site data" or "cookies and site data").
  • Blocking site storage will stop you from staying signed in.
